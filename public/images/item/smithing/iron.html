An iron bar is a metal bar refined through the Smithing skill by smelting two iron ore together, either using a furnace or the Superheat Item spell. Smelting an iron bar requires a Smithing level of 10 and grants 2 Smithing experience. Iron bars can be smelted 25% and 50% faster beginning at Smithing levels 14 and 17, respectively. Additionally, starting at Smithing level 19, there is a chance of smelting an extra bar without consuming additional ores.

The ring of forging enables the smelting of iron bars from only one iron ore.

Iron bars can be forged at an anvil into iron equipment. A list of items produced from iron bars and their requirements may be found here.

Update history

[edit | edit source]
This information has been compiled as part of the update history project. Some updates may not be included – see here for how to help out!
  • update 16 February 2026 (Update):
    • A number of early-game mobs will once again drop actual armour, weapons, ores, and bars.
  • patch 17 May 2021 (Update):
    • The Ring of Forging's effect now works correctly when smelting Iron Bars in New Varrock.
    • Fixed an issue that prevented players with both the Smelting Gauntlets and a Ring of Forging equipped from smelting 60 Iron Bars at once.
  • patch 7 January 2019 (Update):
    • Reduced level requirement to smith iron, steel, mithril, adamant and rune to 10, 20, 30, 40 and 50 respectively.
    • Iron bars now require two iron ores to create.
  • patch 4 March 2013 (Update):
    • Iron no longer appears in the Smithing Skill Advance Guide twice.
  • update 4 January 2001 (Update):
    • Added to game.

Trivia

[edit | edit source]
  • Before the Mining and Smithing rework, noted iron bars could occasionally be found inside barrels. These were replaced with iron stone spirits.
  • Before the Mining and Smithing rework, iron bars required level 15 Smithing and a single iron ore to be smelted. At that level, players had a 50% chance of failing, which would consume the ore without producing a bar. This chance decreased by 1% per level until reaching 20% at level 45. To obtain a 100% smelting success ratio, players needed to use Superheat Item, a ring of forging, the blast furnace, or have level 99 Smithing.
