Raw catfish is a type of fish that can be caught with level 60 Fishing in the Menaphos fishing spots, using a fishing rod and fishing bait. Catching one will award 85 Fishing experience and 4.5 reputation to the faction the player is set as active. Level 60 Cooking is required to cook them and gives 145 Cooking experience.

Catching a raw catfish gives a 1/2,000 chance to receive a slimy egg, which can be used to unlock the Squeck pet.[1]
